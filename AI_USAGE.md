# AI Usage Statement: Level Up Tech Website

## AI Systems Used

- **Gemini (Google):** grammar and wording fixes on the site's text
- **Claude (Anthropic):** help migrating the site from Canva to HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and guidance on domain setup

## How AI Contributed

The original site was designed entirely by me in Canva, including the layout, backgrounds, font choices, and branding. All of the content and business details are mine.

Gemini was used only to correct grammar in text I had already written.

When I learned web development, I rebuilt the site in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Claude assisted with that migration by suggesting ways to structure and optimize the code so the result reads as a real website rather than a slide deck exported to the web, and it gave me step-by-step guidance on domain setup.

AI did not make the design or branding decisions.

## Review Process

I read all AI-assisted output and edited anything that didn't match what I wanted before using it.

Because I work alone on this site, I also asked colleagues in IT to test it, and they caught issues I had missed, such as misspellings and stray content that didn't belong.

## Responsibility

I take full responsibility for the final website, including its accuracy, quality, and appropriateness.

## Context-Specific Considerations

This website was built for my employer, Level Up Tech, as part of my professional work.
