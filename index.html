[ FAQ: QUICK_ANSWERS ]

How long is the warranty?

1 month. Our PCs are brand new (unless we say it's used) and come with a 1-month shop warranty. It covers manufacturer defects only, like a faulty part, rust or corrosion. It does not cover fire or liquid damage, RGB fading or burning out, physical damage, tampering, or user error. Not sure if something's covered? Message us.

Can I return my PC if I don't like it?

Yes. Within 2 days (48 hours) of receiving it, you can return it for a full refund. We don't encourage it and it rarely happens, and you'll need to ship it back yourself.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, through Tamara: split your payment into 4 (it's under maintenance right now, so ask us first). Stripe card payments may also have installment options, with a 3% gateway fee.

Can I sell my old PC and get a new one?

Possible! We'll check your parts first. If they're in good condition and you're happy with our valuation and terms, we can work out a trade-in.

Can I pick up my PC myself?

Yes, and we encourage it. You can ask our IT staff questions and test the PC before you take it home.

Do you have a physical shop?

No. We're an e-trader business licensed by Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), and we operate online only. You're welcome to visit our workshop to pick up or buy a PC, but random visits are discouraged, so message us first. We share the workshop address once a pickup is arranged.

How long does a build take?

24-48 hours, depending on stock and parts availability.

Can I swap parts in a pre-built?

Usually, yes, as long as it's within what we have available and in stock. Ask us on WhatsApp.
